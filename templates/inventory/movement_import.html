Import Stock Movements from CSV

Required columns: sku, movement_type, quantity. movement_type is one of RECEIPT, ISSUE, TRANSFER, ADJUSTMENT. Depending on type, also include: location (Receipt/Issue/Adjustment), source_location + destination_location (Transfer), reason + adjustment_direction (Adjustment, direction is INCREASE or DECREASE). Location values are Location codes (e.g. WH01).

Every row is validated exactly like the manual forms — insufficient stock, a blank adjustment reason, an archived item, or a location you don't have access to will cause that row to be skipped and reported, not silently accepted.
